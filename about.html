About me

I'm a Game Designer with 7+ years in the industry, shipping titles across mobile F2P, MMORPGs, and licensed IP. My path through QA, product management, and design gave me an end-to-end perspective on what it takes to build and ship games.

At Jam City, I went from QA Tester to Game Designer over almost five years, working on Disney IPs including Frozen Adventures, Frozen Free Fall and Disney Emoji Blitz. I've had the chance to collaborate with teams from Canada, Germany, the US, Colombia, Korea, Chile, Mexico and Spain. Most recently, I led quest design and implementation for Forgotten Runiverse, an open-world MMORPG.

I graduated with an Associate Degree in Production and Conceptual Game Design from Image Campus in August 2026, with a 9.5/10 average, and I'm developing personal projects in Godot. I'm looking for systems design, narrative design, or generalist roles at indie and AA studios.

I'm a pen-and-paper-first kind of designer. I sketch systems, map flows, and fill notebooks before anything hits an engine. For me, games have the potential to expand how people experience the world, and I want to build ones worth that potential.

I was born and raised in Buenos Aires, but I've also called Vancouver, Boston, and Montevideo home. When I'm not designing, I'm probably learning about something nerdy, reading with too much coffee, or playing Pokemon with my nephew.
